PI STRUCTURE:
1. HEADER: "PROFORMA INVOICE" centered, bold 16pt. PI Number (PI-YYYYMMDD-XXX) right-aligned below.
2. SELLER DETAILS (left column): Company name, address, tax ID, bank details
3. BUYER DETAILS (right column): Company name, address, contact person
4. ITEMS TABLE (full width, grid borders):
   - Columns: No. | Description | HS Code | Qty | Unit | Unit Price | Total
   - Header row: Times New Roman Bold 10pt, light gray background (#F0F0F0)
   - Last row: "TOTAL" bold, right-aligned amount
5. TERMS SECTION:
   - Incoterms: e.g. "FOB Shanghai, China"
   - Payment Terms: e.g. "30% T/T advance, 70% against copy of B/L"
   - Delivery Time: e.g. "Confirmed at order time based on production schedule and shipping method"
   - Validity: e.g. "This PI is valid until YYYY-MM-DD"
6. BANK DETAILS: Beneficiary name, account number, SWIFT/BIC, bank name and address
7. FOOTER: "This is a Proforma Invoice and not a tax invoice. Generated by CandyPro OEM."
8. SIGNATURE BLOCK: Two columns — "For and on behalf of Seller" | "Accepted by Buyer"
   Each with: Name, Title, Date, Signature line
