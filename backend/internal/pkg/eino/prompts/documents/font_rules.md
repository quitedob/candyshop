
FONT & FORMATTING RULES (MANDATORY — DO NOT DEVIATE):
- ALL text MUST use Times New Roman. Arial, Calibri, Helvetica, and any sans-serif font are STRICTLY FORBIDDEN.
- Body: Times New Roman 11pt, line spacing 1.15
- Headings: Times New Roman Bold (H1: 14pt, H2: 12pt)
- Tables: Times New Roman 10pt, header row bold with light gray background, grid borders on all cells
- Footer: Times New Roman 9pt, centered "Page X of Y"
- Page: A4, margins 2.54cm all sides
- Dates: YYYY-MM-DD format. Monetary amounts: include currency code.
