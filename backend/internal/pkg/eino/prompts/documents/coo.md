CERTIFICATE OF ORIGIN STRUCTURE:
1. HEADER: "CERTIFICATE OF ORIGIN" centered, bold 16pt. Certificate No. COO-YYYYMMDD-XXX.
2. CERTIFYING AUTHORITY section (left): Name and logo of issuing chamber/authority
3. CERTIFICATION STATEMENT: "It is hereby certified that the goods described below are of [Country] origin."
4. GOODS DETAILS:
   - Description of Goods
   - HS Code (6+ digits)
   - Quantity and Unit
   - Gross/Net Weight
   - FTA Type (e.g., FORM E, FORM A, RCEP, GSP)
5. EXPORTER DETAILS: Name, address, country
6. IMPORTER/CONSIGNEE DETAILS: Name, address, country
7. TRANSPORT DETAILS: Vessel/Flight, Port of Loading, Port of Discharge
8. CERTIFICATION: Stamp area, date, authorized signature
9. DECLARATION BY EXPORTER: "The undersigned declares that the above details are correct."
