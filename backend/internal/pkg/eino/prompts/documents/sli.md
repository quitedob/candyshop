SLI STRUCTURE:
1. HEADER: "SHIPPER'S LETTER OF INSTRUCTION" centered, bold 16pt. SLI Number right-aligned.

   - B/L Consignee instructions
   - Notify Party details
   - Special instructions (e.g., "Telex Release", "Express B/L")

8. INSURANCE: Required / Not Required, coverage details
9. SIGNATURE: Shipper's authorized signature and date
