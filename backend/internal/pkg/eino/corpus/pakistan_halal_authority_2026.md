# Pakistan Halal Authority (PHA) Governance Baseline (Official)

## Authority Scope
Pakistan Halal Authority (PHA) is established under an Act of Parliament (Act VIII of 2016) and works under the Ministry of Science and Technology framework for halal trade governance.

## Governance and Registry Signals
- PHA publishes halal governance artifacts, including business and certification-mark related regulations.
- PHA publishes recognized/registered halal certification bodies and related service categories.
- PHA publishes recognized labs and licensed product information streams on its official portal.

## Practical Export Checklist for Halal-Sensitive Orders
- Verify whether the target buyer/country policy requires halal-certified products or process assurance.
- Validate certification chain against currently recognized halal bodies listed by PHA.
- Keep supporting halal documentation aligned with shipment dossier and importer requirements.
