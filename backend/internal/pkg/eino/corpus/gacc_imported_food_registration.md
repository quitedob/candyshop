# GACC: Regulations on the Administration of Registration of Overseas Manufacturers of Imported Food

The "Regulations of the Customs of the People's Republic of China on the Administration of Registration of Overseas Manufacturers of Imported Food" (Decree No. 248 / Decree No. 280) is a key regulation governing the import of food into China, administered by the General Administration of Customs (GAC/GACC).

## Purpose and Scope
*   **Purpose**: To strengthen registration management of overseas manufacturers of imported food, ensuring food safety for products entering China.
*   **Scope**: Applies to overseas production, processing, and storage enterprises exporting food to China.

## Registration Conditions & Procedures
*   Enterprises must be approved and supervised by the competent authority in their country/region and meet China's inspection and quarantine requirements.
*   GAC assesses and reviews the food safety management systems of the exporting country/region.
*   GAC implements classified management for these enterprises based on risk management principles.

## Application Process
*   **Categories**: Some food categories require the overseas manufacturer to be recommended by the competent authority of their country/region to the GAC. Other enterprises may apply directly or through an agent.
*   **Assessment**: GAC organizes expert panels for evaluation (written reviews, video, on-site inspections).
*   **Decision**: GAC issues a registration number in China to approved enterprises. Registration is generally valid for 5 years.

## Labeling Requirements
Registered enterprises exporting food to China must mark their China registration number or the registration number approved by the competent authority of their country/region on the food packaging.

## Post-Registration Supervision
GAC supervises registered overseas food manufacturers. If an enterprise fails to meet requirements, it may be ordered to rectify, and food imports may be suspended during the rectification period.
