# Pakistan PSQCA Mandatory Items Baseline (Official)

## Authority Scope
Pakistan Standards and Quality Control Authority (PSQCA) publishes mandatory items notified under Section 14 of the PSQCA Act VI of 1996.

## Mandatory Standards Relevance for Candy-like Categories
- The PSQCA mandatory items list includes biscuits and wafers biscuits with referenced PS standards.
- Product planning for confectionery-adjacent categories should verify whether the SKU is captured by a mandatory PS standard or related notification before customs and market entry.

## Practical Export Checklist for Candy Orders
- Check PSQCA mandatory list and referenced PS numbers for category coverage.
- Align label and dossier details with importer and regulator expectations before dispatch.
