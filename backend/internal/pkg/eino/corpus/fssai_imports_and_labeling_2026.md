# India FSSAI Imports and Labeling Baseline (Official)

## Authority Scope
FSSAI is the central authority under the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 for imported food compliance in India.

## Import Clearance Workflow (FICS)
- FSSAI states food import clearance is managed through the Food Import Clearance System (FICS).
- FICS is integrated with Customs ICEGATE under SWIFT.
- Imported food referred by customs is subject to document scrutiny, visual inspection, sampling, and testing.
- Outcome is typically NOC (conforming) or NCR (non-conforming) in the FICS process.

## Labeling and Display Regulatory Track
- FSSAI maintains Food Safety and Standards (Labelling and Display) Regulations, 2020.
- FSSAI publishes amendment timelines for these regulations, including the 2025 amendment cycle.
- Exporters should verify latest amendment text before finalizing retail or customs-facing labels.

## Practical Export Checklist for Candy Orders
- Confirm importer-side FICS readiness and document package before dispatch.
- Validate product standards/additives against FSS regulations applicable to confectionery category.
- Validate labeling against the latest Labelling and Display regulation amendments.
- Keep an appeal/remediation path prepared for NCR scenarios in customs-linked clearance.
